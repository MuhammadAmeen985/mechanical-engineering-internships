# 🏗️ Mechanical Engineering Internships

A curated collection of mechanical engineering internship opportunities, career resources, and application guidance for students and recent graduates.

## 📌 About This Repository

This repository is designed to help mechanical engineering students discover internship opportunities, organize their job search, and prepare for careers in engineering and related industries.

It serves as a central resource for exploring internships in mechanical design, manufacturing, thermal systems, HVAC, robotics, automation, and other mechanical engineering disciplines.

## 🎯 Objectives

- 🔎 Collect and organize mechanical engineering internship opportunities.
- 🏭 Explore opportunities across different engineering industries.
- 🌍 Identify local and international internship programs.
- 📄 Share application tips, résumé guidance, and interview preparation resources.
- 🚀 Help students connect academic knowledge with practical engineering experience.

## 🔍 Where to Find Internships

Explore these platforms to search for opportunities:

- [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/)
- [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/)
- [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Job/)
- [Handshake](https://joinhandshake.com/) — opportunities for eligible students and graduates
- [Internship listings on Google](https://www.google.com/search?q=mechanical+engineering+internships)
- Company-specific career pages and university career portals

International applicants should verify work authorization, visa sponsorship, and location requirements before applying.
